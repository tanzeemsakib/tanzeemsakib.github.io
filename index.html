About

I'm a product designer and visual artist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, working across branding, illustration, motion, and UI. My work tends toward the editorial — restrained palettes, deliberate composition, ideas that stay with you.

Alongside design, I have a background in applied mathematics at the University of Dhaka — which informs how I approach problems: systematically, but with room for the unexpected.

Associated with Shundor Studio as a Design Associate. Most recently worked with Activate Rights on motion and social media assets for Archive & Resist Conclave 2026 — a two-day international convening that brought together archivists, human rights defenders, and civic technologists from across the UK, USA, Indonesia, Kenya, South Africa, and beyond, across 19 parallel sessions and 4 plenaries. Also edited video content for Fahim Explains, growing the platform from 800–2K average views to peaks of 120K and 112K — 600K+ total views across 14 edited posts.

Currently working at Studio Shwo in the Design wing. Open to freelance commissions and full-time opportunities.
